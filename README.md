## 🚀 About Me

Astrophysicist, startup founder, and Anthropic community ambassador in Colombia, with over a decade in Latin America's technology ecosystem. At Platzi he built the platform's first machine learning systems and led the data science team; as co-founder of Hunty he built an AI system that lets large enterprises automate their recruiting and selection processes intelligently and at scale, earning recognition from accelerator programs including Google for Startups and the AWS Generative AI Startup Program. His trajectory reflects a continuous effort to unify the best of all his worlds — scientist, consultant, and startup founder — turning complex technical knowledge into tangible products that impact the lives of millions.
